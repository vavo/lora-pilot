First approve a reference, then review the variations. Identity checks are suggestions; look closely at faces before keeping an image.

Prompt input creates four SDXL references. Variations use Qwen Image Edit, with up to three attempts per image. Local captions and visual checks run on CPU and may take a long time for larger sets. Images stay on your pod; GPU time is still billed by your host. Use photos you have permission to use.
